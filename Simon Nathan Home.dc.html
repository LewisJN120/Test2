BIO

Simon is an orchestrator whose work spans film, television and the stage, including Emilia Pérez (Netflix), for which "El Mal" - a song he co-orchestrated - won the 2025 Academy Award and Golden Globe for Best Original Song.

He has orchestrated for studios such as Netflix, Warner Bros., Disney Interactive, HBO and Pathé. He has worked for composers including Alan Menken, Jason Robert Brown, Matthew Wilder (Disney's Mulan), Howard Goodall, Clément Ducol, and Joseph Trapanese.
